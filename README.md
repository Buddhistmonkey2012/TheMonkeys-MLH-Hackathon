Our Problem: When trying to help people to pursue their carrer in the tech side, this non profit gives them mentoring to help them acheive their goal. The problem with this is that sometimes the mentees dont come up to a lot of the meetings which is bad and a lot of things get mixed up and a lot of confusion.

Our solution is we have a smooth website that helps with communication between the mentors and the mentees. It also has a schedule where u can track when the meetings are and you can set a reminder. It also has a public schedule for other events and things. Based on your story, we have a place where its shows the most recommended mentor for you! After your meetings, you can schedule a follow up of when you can do the next meeting. When things change, you can update you profile too.
